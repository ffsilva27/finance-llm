# Documento Financeiro 146301

CAIRO, Feb 28 (Reuters) - Egypt’s M2 money supply was up 20.45 percent at the end of January from a year earlier, central bank data showed on Wednesday. The money supply stood at 3.25 trillion Egyptian pounds ($185 billion), the bank said. ($1 = 17.6000 Egyptian pounds) (Reporting by Nadine Awadalla Editing by Robin Pomeroy)
