# Documento Financeiro 130319

Feb 20 (Reuters) - SYDBANK A/S: * SYDBANK TO REPURCHASE SHARES * ‍DECIDED TO IMPLEMENT A SHARE BUYBACK PROGRAMME OF DKK 500M, HOWEVER A MAXIMUM OF 3 MILLION SHARES.​ * USING CLOSING PRICE ON MONDAY 19 FEB BUYBACK EQUALS ABOUT 1.9M SHARES, EQUAL TO 2.8% OF BANK‘S SHARE CAPITAL * ‍PURPOSE OF SHARE BUYBACK IS TO REDUCE BANK‘S SHARE CAPITAL WITH SHARES PURCHASED UNDER PROGRAMME​ * ‍SHARE BUYBACK PROGRAMME WILL BE INITIATED ON WEDNESDAY 21 FEBRUARY 2018 AND COMPLETED BY 31 DEC 2018​ Source text for Eikon: Further company coverage: (Gdynia Newsroom)
