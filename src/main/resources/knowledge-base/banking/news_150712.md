# Documento Financeiro 150712

An attempt to quickly sell Weinstein Co. assets in bankruptcy is facing pushback over how lawsuits pending against the company will be handled. The troubled TV and film studio filed for chapter 11 protection late Monday in Delaware with a deal in hand to sell its assets to investment firm Lantern Capital for $310 million, subject to better offers in a potential bankruptcy auction. Weinstein Co. advisers want a judge to approve a sale by May 4, court papers say. ...
