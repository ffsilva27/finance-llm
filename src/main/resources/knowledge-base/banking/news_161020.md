# Documento Financeiro 161020

March 5 (Reuters) - Punjab National Bank: * SBI HONG KONG SAYS DOES NOT HAVE ANY EXPOSURE AGAINST ANY CORPORATES/BANKS BASED UPON LOUS/LCS ISSUED BY PNB, BRADY HOUSE BRANCH * SBI HONG KONG BRANCH DOES NOT HAVE ANY EXPOSURE TO NIRAV MODI GROUP OF COS AND / OR MEHUL CHOKSI GROUP OF COS Source text - We refer to the news dated 18th February 2018 & 19th February 2018 reported by some sections of media and advise that SBI Hong Kong Branch does not have any exposure against any corporates / banks based upon LOUs / LCs issued by PNB, Brady House Branch. Further, SBI Hong Kong Branch does not have any exposure to the Nirav Modi Group of companies and / or Mehul Choksi group of companies whose names are linked to the PNB fraud.
