# Documento Financeiro 137168

March 20, 2018 / 5:44 PM / Updated an hour ago JPMorgan names Mellody Hobson to board Reuters Staff 1 Min Read March 20 (Reuters) - JPMorgan & Chase Co has appointed Mellody Hobson, the president of a Chicago-based investment firm, to its board of directors, the Wall Street bank said here on Tuesday. Crandall Bowles will retire from the board and will not stand for re-election. Hobson also serves on the boards of Starbucks and Estee Lauder Companies. (Reporting by Parikshit Mishra in Bengaluru)
