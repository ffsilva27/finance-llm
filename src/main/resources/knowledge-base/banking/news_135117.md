# Documento Financeiro 135117

March 13, 2018 / 9:47 AM / Updated 3 hours ago EU states agree on clamp-down on accountants helping aggressive tax planning Reuters Staff 1 Min Read BRUSSELS (Reuters) - European Union finance ministers agreed on Tuesday new measures to force accountants and banks to report aggressive tax schemes that help companies shift profits to low-tax countries, in a bid to tackle tax avoidance. “We have adopted” the new rules, Bulgaria’s finance minister Vladislav Goranov told a public session of a meeting in which all ministers supported the overhaul. Information on harmful tax planning will also be shared among the 28 EU states. Reporting by Francesco Guarascio
