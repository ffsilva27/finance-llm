Here's What Elizabeth Warren Thinks About Wells Fargo CEO Tim Sloan's Pay Raise Sen. Elizabeth Warren holds a news conference on banking deregulation legislation on March 6, 2018. Bill Clark—CQ-Roll Call/Getty Images By Bloomberg 1:08 AM EDT Wells Fargo & Co. (wfc) Chief Executive Officer Tim Sloan’s pay raise drew criticism from U.S. Senator Elizabeth Warren. “Wells Fargo is about to be sanctioned for running an auto-insurance scam that cost Americans millions of dollars. I don’t think that sort of corporate management merits a raise for CEO Tim Sloan,” Warren, a Massachusetts Democrat and frequent Wall Street critic, said Thursday in a tweet . The San Francisco-based bank has been embroiled in scandals for 16 months, including a revelation that auto-loan clients were forced to pay for unwanted car insurance . The firm announced this week that it paid Sloan $17.4 million for 2017, a 36% increase from a year earlier. For more on Sen.
