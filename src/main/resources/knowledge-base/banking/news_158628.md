# Documento Financeiro 158628

March 16, 2018 / 6:50 PM / Updated 14 minutes ago MOVES-Bank of America's EMEA head to leave -memo Reuters Staff 1 Min Read NEW YORK, March 16 (Reuters) - Alex Wilmot-Sitwell, president of Bank of America Merrill Lynch’s Europe, Middle East and Africa region, is leaving the bank, according to an internal memo seen by Reuters on Friday. Wilmot-Sitwell, who has led the EMEA business for the last six years, will be leaving in coming months for outside opportunities, according to a memo from the bank’s chief operating officer Tom Montag. (Reporting by Catherine Ngai; Editing by Meredith Mazzilli)
