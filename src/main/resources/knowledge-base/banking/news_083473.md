# Documento Financeiro 83473

MUMBAI, Feb 12 (Reuters) - The Reserve Bank of India said late on Monday it was withdrawing all its existing mechanisms for tackling bad debt at Indian banks and replacing them with a harmonised and simplified generic framework for resolving stressed assets. The new system will force lenders to identify and tackle any stressed-asset accounts more rapidly, the regulator said. Last year, India tweaked its laws to give the central bank more authority to push lenders to deal with the nearly $150 billion in troubled debt at banks, which has choked off new lending and slowed the economy. (Reporting by Devidutta Tripathy and Euan Rocha, editing by Larry King)
