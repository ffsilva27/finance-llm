# Documento Financeiro 119633

Swiss central bank has no plan for digital currency – Zurbruegg February 14, 2018 FRANKFURT, Feb 14 (Reuters) – The Swiss National Bank is not planning to issue a digital currency, which would give holders a direct claim on the central bank, board member Fritz Zurbruegg said on Wednesday. “The Swiss National Bank is not planning the issue of a so called central bank digital currency,” he told a conference organised by the Bundesbank. (Reporting By Francesco Canepa; Editing by Alison Williams)
