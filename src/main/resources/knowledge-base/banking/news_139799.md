# Documento Financeiro 139799

March 2, 2018 / 3:02 PM / Updated 29 minutes ago Equifax expects net $200 million in breach-related costs in 2018 Reuters Staff 1 Min Read NEW YORK (Reuters) - Equifax Inc said on Friday it expects $275 million (199.67 million pounds) in costs in 2018 related to the credit reporting company’s massive data breach last year, offset by $75 million in insurance proceeds. FILE PHOTO: Credit reporting company Equifax Inc. corporate offices are pictured in Atlanta, Georgia, U.S., September 8, 2017. REUTERS/Tami Chappell/File Photo The costs mainly reflect technology and data security upgrades, legal fees, and the offering of free identity theft protection and credit monitoring services to the more than 147 million consumers who were affected by the cybersecurity incident.
