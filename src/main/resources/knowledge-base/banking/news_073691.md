# Documento Financeiro 73691

LONDON—HSBC Holdings PLC’s full-year profit missed analysts’ expectations after the bank was hit by the high-profile collapses of two borrowers in the U.K. and South Africa. Debt related to South African retailer Steinhoff International Holdings NV and U.K. services and construction company Carillion PLC helped push the bank’s bad loan charges to $1.77 billion for the year, more than expected.
