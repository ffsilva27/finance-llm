# Documento Financeiro 160742

CAIRO, March 1 (Reuters) - Average yields on Egypt’s six-month and one-year treasury bills rose at an auction on Thursday, data from the central bank showed. The yield on the 182-day bill rose to 17.907 percent from 17.71 percent at the last similar auction, and the yield on the 364-day bill rose to 16.894 percent from 16.826 percent. (Reporting by Arwa Gaballa Editing by Matthew Mpoke Bigg)
