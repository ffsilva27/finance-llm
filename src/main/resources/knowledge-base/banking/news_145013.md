# Documento Financeiro 145013

March 21, 2018 / 8:03 AM / a few seconds ago Troubled UK retailer Carpetright to raise capital, sell stake to key investor Reuters Staff 1 Min Read (Reuters) - British retailer Carpetright ( CPRC.L ) has agreed a loan with a shareholder in exchange for new shares to fund the short term running of the company, and may raise a further 60 million pounds ($84.15 million) to strengthen its balance sheet, it said on Wednesday. Carpetright, which has issued a series of profit-warnings because of tepid high-street spending, said it would also look to reduce its property portfolio. It has agreed the loan with shareholder Meditor. Reporting by Elisabeth O'Leary; Editing by Kate Holton
