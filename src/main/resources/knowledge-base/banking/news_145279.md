# Documento Financeiro 145279

BURBANK, Calif.--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- The Walt Disney Company (NYSE: DIS) will discuss fiscal second quarter 2018 financial results via a live audio webcast beginning at 4:30 p.m. EDT / 1:30 p.m. PDT on Tuesday, May 8, 2018. Results will be released after the close of regular trading on May 8, 2018. To listen to the webcast, please visit www.disney.com/investors . The webcast presentation will be archived.
