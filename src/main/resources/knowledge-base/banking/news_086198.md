# Documento Financeiro 86198

RED BANK, N.J., Jan. 31, 2018 /PRNewswire/ -- The Trustees of North European Oil Royalty Trust (NYSE-NRT) announced today a quarterly distribution of $0.17 per unit for the first quarter of fiscal 2018, payable on February 28, 2018 to holders of record on February 16, 2018. Natural gas sold during the fourth calendar quarter of 2017 is the primary source of royalty income on which the February 2018 distribution is based. John R. Van Kirk, Managing Director, reported that this year's quarterly distribution of $0.17 per unit is 13.33%, or $0.02 per unit, higher than the distribution of $0.15 per unit for the first quarter of fiscal 2017. Specific details will be available in the earnings press release scheduled for publication on or about February 16, 2018.
