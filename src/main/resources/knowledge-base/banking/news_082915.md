# Documento Financeiro 82915

(Adds new role) LONDON, Feb 15 (IFR) - Harsh Srivastav left Morgan Stanley’s high-yield and leveraged loan syndicate at the start of the week, according to sources. A vice-president on the team in London since January 2016, Srivastav joined the US bank in 2011 as an analyst, according to his LinkedIn profile. The sources said Srivastav is taking up a role with PIMCO as a portfolio manager, starting in May. Morgan Stanley declined to comment. (Reporting by Yoruk Bahceli, editing by Alex Chambers)
