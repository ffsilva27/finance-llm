# Documento Financeiro 121648

Bank lending is once again off to a very slow start in 2018, but this time it is likely a temporary slowdown amid growing corporate confidence. Total commercial and industrial loans extended by U.S. banks were up just 1% from a year earlier on Feb. 7, according to weekly Federal Reserve data. For the month of January, C&I loans were down an annualized 10.8% compared to December, according to calculations by Keefe, Bruyette and Woods. ...
