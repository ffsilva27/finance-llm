LONDON, Feb 20 (Reuters) - Britain lawmakers said on Tuesday they have published in full a report by British regulators into how Royal Bank of Scotland allegedly mistreated small businesses. The Treasury Select Committee said in a statement it has agreed to publish the final, unredacted report immediately. Reporting by Huw Jones, editing by Louise Heavens
