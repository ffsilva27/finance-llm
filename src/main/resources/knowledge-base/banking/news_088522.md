# Documento Financeiro 88522

YEREVAN, Feb 13 (Reuters) - Armenia’s central bank said on Tuesday it had decided to keep its key refinancing rate unchanged at 6 percent. Consumer prices in Armenia rose by 2.7 percent in January month-on-month compared with 1.5 percent in December 2017, and by 2.7 percent in year-on-year terms as well.
