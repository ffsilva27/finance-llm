# Documento Financeiro 147787

March 21, 2018 / 7:31 AM / Updated 24 minutes ago BOK chief sees low likelihood of U.S. labelling S.Korea as currency manipulator Reuters Staff 1 Min Read SEOUL, March 21 (Reuters) - South Korea’s central bank chief said on Wednesday that the probability of the country being labelled by the United States as a currency manipulator seemed low. Bank of Korea’s Governot Lee Ju-yeol told a confirmation hearing that active discussions with the United States over currencies were ongoing. (Reporting by Dahee Kim Editing by Eric Meijer)
