# Documento Financeiro 116942

42 AM / in a minute Russia's VTB bank says sells some Burger King shares Reuters Staff 1 Min Read MOSCOW, Feb 26 (Reuters) - VTB, Russia’s second biggest lender, has sold some of its shares in fast food chain Burger King, owned by Restaurant Brands International Inc , VTB Senior Vice President Dmitry Pyanov said on Monday. Pyanov did not disclose the buyer. (Reporting by Tatiana Voronova; Writing by Polina Nikolskaya; Editing by Polina Ivanova)
