# Documento Financeiro 166518

TOKYO—SoftBank GroupCorp. could relist U.K.-based chip designer Arm Holdings PLC within 12 years, a senior executive said. Yoshimitsu Goto, a senior corporate officer at SoftBank, told investors at the Credit Suisse Asian Investment Conference in Hong Kong that an initial public offering of Arm was one possible exit strategy for the conglomerate’s $92 billion Vision Fund, a U.S. fund manager said, confirming a report by the Financial Times. Mr. Goto added there was no concrete plan to list Arm, the person said. ...
