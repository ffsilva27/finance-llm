# Documento Financeiro 137544

Your editorial “Tackling China’s Protectionism” (March 21) discusses several of China’s “predatory” policies, and then concludes that “all of these policies violate World Trade Organization agreements.” Unfortunately, that’s not true as the most relevant WTO rules—written in 1994 and for China in 2001—don’t prohibit the behavior being complained about. Identifying “strategic emerging industries” and giving them loans on easy terms isn’t necessarily a violation of the WTO subsidies agreement.
