# Documento Financeiro 109683

NASHVILLE, Tenn.,Feb. 20, 2018(GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Buffkin / Baker, a leading national retained search firm, is pleased to announce that Ted Jadick – a veteran of 40 years of building strong corporate boards of directors – has joined Buffkin/Baker as a partner and head of the firm’s global governance practice. Jadick Headshot Before joining Buffkin/Baker in January, Jadick was a vice chairman of Heidrick & Struggles, a member of the firm’s executive committee and the managing partner of its New York Office. “I am very excited about the opportunity to build a new practice at Buffkin/Baker, a firm which clearly understands the importance of board diversity, social responsibility, shareholder activism, and sophisticated fiduciary oversight in today’s global business environment,” Jadick said. His client list includes Major League Baseball, Office Depot, Abercrombie & Fitch, DIRECTV, Dollar General, Hershey Trust Company, PricewaterhouseCoopers, Tiffany & Co., Unilever NV, ConEdison, PPL Corporation, Bristol Myers Squibb, Webster Bank, and Lands’ End. Jadick was nominated by clients as one of the top executive recruiters in America in all three editions of John Sibbald's Career Makers. He is also listed among the top 200 executive recruiters in the world in The Global 200 Executive Recruiters by Nancy Garrison Jenn. Before joining Heidrick, Jadick was senior vice president at Frank W. Hastings. He previously served as a staff accountant and auditor at Haskins and Sells (Deloitte), and began his career at Arthur Young & Company. A former trustee of the University of Scranton, Jadick was recently inducted into the Kania School of Management's Business Leaders Hall of Fame. He is also a member of the University's President's Business Council. He holds a bachelor’s degree in accounting from the University of Scranton. Jadick is a former Trustee of Calvary Hospital Fund, Bronx New York. “Ted brings a career in executive search that spans several decades. He was instrumental in building the corporate governance practice at Heidrick as well as serving in several leadership roles. We are excited to have Ted on board to establish a board practice at Buffkin/Baker and to leverage his extensive background as we continue to build the firm and the New York office,” said Craig Buffkin, managing partner of Buffkin / Baker. “His diverse roster of clients and keen understanding of how boards lead in today’s business environment positions our firm for great future success.” Attachments: A photo accompanying this announcement is available at http://www.globenewswire.com/NewsRoom/AttachmentNg/8ea04ecf-e0cc-44ce-9d65-cb60cb871be2 Attachments: A photo accompanying this announcement is available at http://www.globenewswire.com/NewsRoom/AttachmentNg/a3987c6b-e1a0-4b0a-8389-b350eb576714 Todd Smith Buffkin / Baker 6154545745 todd@deanesmith.agency Source: Buffkin / Baker
