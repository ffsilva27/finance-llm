Latvian PM says central bank chief should resign Monday, February 19, 2018 - 01:28 The head of Latvia’s central bank should resign following his detention by the anti-corruption agency, Prime Minister Maris Kucinskis said on Monday. As Ciara Lee reports, it comes after Latvia’s Corruption Prevention and Combating Bureau detained Governor Ilmars Rimsevics on Saturday after searching his house and office.
