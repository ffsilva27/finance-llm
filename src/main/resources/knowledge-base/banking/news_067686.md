# Documento Financeiro 67686

A growing number of companies are paying employees to get their financial lives in order, reflecting concern over the impact money problems are having on employees’ stress and productivity levels. Aetna Inc., Pitt Ohio LLC, SunTrust Banks Inc. and others are handing out cash and other inducements to workers who take steps to shore up their finances by reducing debt, funding emergency-savings accounts, and attending financial-education classes and meetings with advisers. ...
