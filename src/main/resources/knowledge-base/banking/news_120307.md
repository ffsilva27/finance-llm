# Documento Financeiro 120307

LONDON (Reuters) - HSBC Holdings has named Charlie Nunn as chief executive of its retail banking and wealth management business, the bank said on Wednesday. Nunn replaces John Flint who will take over as the British lender’s overall chief executive on Feb. 21. Nunn joined the bank in 2011 and is already acting head of HSBC’s retail banking and wealth management business. Reporting by Lawrence White; editing by Jason Neely
