# Documento Financeiro 77008

WASHINGTON--The U.S. student-loan program is rapidly headed toward becoming a net cost to the federal government, reversing years of projected profits, according to a government report released Friday. The report, from the Education Department’s inspector general, said the financial squeeze was a result of Americans enrolling in government debt-forgiveness plans.
