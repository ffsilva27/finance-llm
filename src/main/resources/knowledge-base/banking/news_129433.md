# Documento Financeiro 129433

February 12, 2018 / 9:43 AM / Updated 14 minutes ago Lloyds Banking Group sets targets for ethnic minority representation Reuters Staff 1 Lloyds Banking Group said on Monday it would aim to fill 8 percent of its senior management roles with people from ethnic minority backgrounds by 2020, becoming the first FTSE 100 company to set a public target. Currently 5.6 percent of the bank’s senior management and 8.3 percent of the bank’s entire workforce are from black and minority ethnic (BAME) backgrounds.
