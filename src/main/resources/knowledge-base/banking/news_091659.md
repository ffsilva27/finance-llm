# Documento Financeiro 91659

TOKYO, Feb 9 (Reuters) - The Japanese government has decided to nominate Bank of Japan Governor Haruhiko Kuroda to serve as central bank chief for another term when his current one expires in April, Kyodo news agency reported on Friday. The nomination would need approval by both houses of parliament. (Reporting by Chang-Ran Kim; editing by Andrew Roche)
