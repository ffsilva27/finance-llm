# Documento Financeiro 71831

February 20, 2018 / 7:24 AM / Updated 10 minutes ago Slovenia ice hockey player Jeglic fails doping test, to leave Games - CAS PYEONGCHANG, South Korea (Reuters) - Slovenian ice hockey player Ziga Jeglic has tested positive for a banned substance and been suspended for the remainder of the Pyeongchang Winter Olympics, the Court of Arbitration for Sport said on Tuesday. Jeglic, who tested positive for fenoterol, has accepted the anti-doping rule violation and will leave the Olympics within 24 hours, CAS said. Fenoterol can be taken for asthma as it facilitates breathing by opening up the airways. Jeglic, who scored Slovenia’s shootout winner against Slovakia on Saturday, failed an in-competition test and the final decision on his sanction will come after the Games. It has been prescribed to me after testing for respiratory problems in Slovakia in 2017,” Jeglic said in a statement.
