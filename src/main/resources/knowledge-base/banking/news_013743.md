# Documento Financeiro 13743

Retirement specialists say most employers should stick with automatic enrollment into 401(k) plans despite new academic research that finds the practice appears to be driving up mortgage and auto debt among participants. Neil Lloyd, head of U.S. defined contribution and financial wellness research at consulting firm Mercer, said the findings aren’t a total surprise. He cites a client with high employee turnover that recently added auto-enrollment only to reverse course after the number of 401(k) loans increased. ...
