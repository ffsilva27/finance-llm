# Documento Financeiro 139836

DALLAS, March 6, 2018 /PRNewswire/ -- Alerian announced today the 19 winners of the 2018 Ammys, recognizing excellence in the North American energy infrastructure industry. These individuals and companies, along with the previously announced Ammys Hall of Fame Class of 2018 inductees, were recognized at the Third Annual Ammys Dinner, hosted by Kevin Howard on March 5 at the Crystal Ballroom in Houston, Texas. Alerian also awarded its first-ever Medal of Distinction to US Senator John Cornyn for his work on the Tax Cuts and Jobs Act in support of Master Limited Partnerships (MLPs). "As the Senate worked to pass the Tax Cuts and Jobs Act, we took care to ensure the [Internal Revenue] Code would support our job creators," Cornyn said. "Thanks to everyone in the energy community for working hard to make sure that one day we can be energy independent." A complete list of winners and previously announced Hall of Fame inductees is below. Additional information about the event, including presenters and finalists, can be found at ammyawards.com .
