# Documento Financeiro 68716

MUMBAI (Reuters) - The Reserve Bank of India said on Friday it has “undertaken a supervisory assessment of control systems” at and will take “appropriate supervisory action” after the lender disclosed this week it had been hit by a $1.77 billion scam. The RBI also denied a report from The Economic Times newspaper that PNB had been asked to assume all liabilities arising from the fraud, according to a statement. “RBI denies having given any such instructions,” the statement said.
