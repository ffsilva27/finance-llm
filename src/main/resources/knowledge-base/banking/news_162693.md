March 15, 2018 / 7:02 PM / in 11 hours Thiem withdraws from Miami Open due to injury Reuters Staff 1 World number six Dominic Thiem has withdrawn from the Miami Open due to an ankle injury, with the Austrian hoping to return for the claycourt season in Europe next month. Mar 10, 2018; Indian Wells, CA, USA; Dominic Thiem (AUT) during his second round match against Stefanos Tsitsipas (not pictured) in the BNP Paribas Open at the Indian Wells Tennis Garden. Mandatory Credit: Jayne Kamin-Oncea-USA TODAY Sports The 24-year-old retired while trailing Pablo Cuevas 6-3 4-6 2-4 during his third round match at Indian Wells earlier this week. “Unfortunately I have to pull out from Miami. Hopefully I will be 100 percent again on the clay in Europe,” Thiem said on his official Instagram account. Thiem joins a list of top players, including Rafael Nadal and Stan Wawrinka, to withdraw from the Miami Open, which is held from March 21 to April 1.
