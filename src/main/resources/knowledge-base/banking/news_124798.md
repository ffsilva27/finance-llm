# Documento Financeiro 124798

Feb 5 (Reuters) - CREDIT BANK OF MOSCOW: * SAYS HAS INVITED PAVEL SHEVCHUK TO THE POSITION OF FIRST DEPUTY CHAIRMAN OF THE MANAGEMENT BOARD * PAVEL SHEVCHUK IS GOING TO SUPERVISE CORPORATE AND RETAIL BUSINESS OF CREDIT BANK OF MOSCOW * UPON AUTHORISATION BY THE BANK OF RUSSIA, SHEVCHUK WILL BECOME A MEMBER OF THE MANAGEMENT BOARD AS FIRST DEPUTY CHAIRMAN OF THE MANAGEMENT BOARD * NOW HE SERVES AS ADVISOR TO THE CHAIRMAN OF THE MANAGEMENT BOARD * DMITRY EREMIN, WHO HAS HELD THE OFFICE OF FIRST DEPUTY CHAIRMAN OF THE MANAGEMENT BOARD, DECIDED TO LEAVE THE BANK ON FEB. 15 Source text for Eikon: Further company coverage: (Gdynia Newsroom)
