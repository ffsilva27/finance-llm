# Documento Financeiro 83005

DUBLIN, Feb 19 (Reuters) - Ireland’s Central Bank on Monday directed CBL Insurance Europe, a wholly owned subsidiary of New Zealand’s CBL Corp Ltd, to cease writing new business with immediate effect while it works through a number of supervisory issues. CBL Insurance Europe, which underwrites construction-related insurance, continues to operate and existing policies continue to remain in force, the central bank said in a statement. CBL Insurance Europe is registered in Ireland and authorised to write business in Ireland and on a freedom of services basis in Belgium, Denmark, France, Italy, Norway, Romania, Spain, Sweden and Britain. (Reporting by Padraic Halpin; Editing by Adrian Croft)
