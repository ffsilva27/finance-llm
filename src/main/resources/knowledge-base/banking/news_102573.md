# Documento Financeiro 102573

LUANDA, Jan 31 (Reuters) - Angola’s net foreign exchange reserves fell to $13.3 billion in December from $14.25 billion in November, data posted on the central bank’s website showed on Wednesday. (Writing by Olivia Kumwenda-Mtambo; Editing by Alexander Winning)
