# Documento Financeiro 87331

BOCA RATON, Fla., Feb. 09, 2018 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- ADT Inc. (NYSE:ADT) will release its fourth quarter and full year 2017 financial results before trading begins on Thursday, March 15, 2018. Management will host a conference call on the day of the release (March 15, 2018) at 8:30 am ET to discuss ADT’s financial results. The conference call can be accessed as follows: By dialing 1-877-407-3982 (domestic) or 1-201-493-6780 (international) and requesting the ADT Fourth Quarter and Full Year 2017 Earnings Conference Call Live webcast accessed through ADT’s website at investor.adt.com . An audio replay of the conference call will be available from approximately 11:30 am ET on March 15, 2018 until 11:59 pm ET on March 29, 2018, and can be accessed by dialing 1-844-512-2921 (domestic) or 1-412-317-6671 (international), and providing the passcode 13676564, or by accessing ADT's website at www.investor.adt.com . About ADT ADT is a leading provider of security and automation solutions for homes and businesses in the United States and Canada, people on-the-go and their networks. Making security more accessible than ever before, and backed by 24/7 customer support, ADT is committed to providing superior customer service with a focus on speed and quality of responsiveness, helping customers feel more safe and empowered. ADT is headquartered in Boca Raton, Florida and employs more than 18,000 people throughout North America.
