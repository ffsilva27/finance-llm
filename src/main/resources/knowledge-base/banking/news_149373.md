# Documento Financeiro 149373

ZURICH—Credit Suisse Group AG Chief Executive Tidjane Thiam was awarded 9.7 million Swiss francs ($10.2 million) in compensation for 2017, as his strategic shift toward managing money for wealthy clients showed signs of paying off despite the bank posting a third-straight annual loss. Total compensation for the bank’s 12-member executive board came in at 69.9 million francs last year, down about three million francs from 2016, while the total bonus pool was 3.19 billion francs, up 3% from 2016. ...
