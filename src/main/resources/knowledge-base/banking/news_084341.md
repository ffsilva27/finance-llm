# Documento Financeiro 84341

CAIRO, Jan 31 (Reuters) - Egypt’s M2 money supply was up 20.919 percent at the end of December from a year earlier, central bank data showed on Wednesday. The money supply stood at 3.2 trillion Egyptian pounds ($181.33 billion), the bank said. ($1 = 17.6475 Egyptian pounds) (Reporting by Nadine Awadalla; Writing by Arwa Gaballa; Editing by Peter Graff)
