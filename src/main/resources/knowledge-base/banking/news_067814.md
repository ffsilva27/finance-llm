# Documento Financeiro 67814

BEIJING, Feb 1 (Reuters) - The value of assets held by China’s banking sector rose 8.6 percent on-year to 245.78 trillion yuan ($39.05 trillion) as of end-December, the China Banking Regulatory Commission said on Thursday. Liabilities also rose 8.3 percent from a year earlier to 226.37 trillion yuan, it said on its website. ($1 = 6.2935 Chinese yuan renminbi) (Reporting by Beijing Monitoring Desk; Editing by Shri Navaratnam)
