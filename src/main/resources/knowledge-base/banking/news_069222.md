# Documento Financeiro 69222

WASHINGTON (Reuters) - Federal Reserve Chairman Jerome Powell will testify on the central bank’s semiannual report on monetary policy and the economy on Feb. 28 before the House of Representatives’ Financial Services Committee, the panel announced on Tuesday. The hearing with Powell, who took office on Monday, will be held at 10 a.m. (1500 GMT), the committee said. Reporting by Tim Ahmann; Editing by Eric Beech
