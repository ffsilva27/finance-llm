# Documento Financeiro 92288

MOUNT PLEASANT, S.C., Feb. 2, 2018 /PRNewswire/ -- Wayfinder Holdings, LLC is pleased to announce the acquisition of Autumn Cove Assisted Living and Memory Care, a 65-unit senior living community in Anniston, Alabama. Eric Hadley, President of Wayfinder Management, says Autumn Cove is simply a great fit. Mr. Hadley goes on to say that Autumn Cove is a well-respected, single site community that will nicely be in tandem with our structure and culture and we are excited to grow with this level of intention. The acquisition took place on January 1, 2018 and involved the following, well respected senior living acquisition team: Brokerage Services: Senturian Senior Housing Brokerage Watson McCollister, Broker Legal Representation: Baker Donelson Richard Faulkner, Lead Counsel Financing Representation: Lancaster Pollard Gerald Swiacki, Vice President Lending: United Community Bank Denis Rowlend, Vice President Contemporary Healthcare Capital Steve McGee, Partner About Wayfinder: Based in Mount Pleasant, South Carolina, Wayfinder owns, manages and develops senior living communities. Wayfinder seeks intentional growth with a desire to preserve local community presence and identity. The Southeast is our primary geography. Senior living; including independent living, assisted living and memory care, is our core purpose. www.wayfindermgmt.com .
