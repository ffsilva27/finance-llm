# Documento Financeiro 93263

JOHANNESBURG, Feb 27 (Reuters) - South African lender FirstRand said on Tuesday group Chief Executive Johan Burger will retire at the end of March and be succeeded by the company’s group deputy CEO Alan Pullinger. Pullinger will take over at the helm from April 1, the banking group said in a statement. (Reporting by Nqobile Dludla Editing by Ed Stoddard)
