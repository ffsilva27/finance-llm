# Documento Financeiro 168662

Japan’s SoftBank Group Corp. is looking to invest $1 billion in a Chinese truck-hailing company backed by internet giant Tencent Holdings Ltd. and a private-equity firm co-founded by billionaire Jack Ma, according to people familiar with the matter. The possible investment is part of a fundraising effort by Manbang Group, a tech unicorn that runs a mobile app platform matching truck drivers with shippers who have cargo to move.
