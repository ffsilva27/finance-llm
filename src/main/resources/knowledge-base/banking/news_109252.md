# Documento Financeiro 109252

COPENHAGEN, Feb 2 (Reuters) - Danske Bank’s fourth-quarter pretax profit came in at 6.73 billion Danish crowns ($1.13 billion), beating the 6.45 billion expected in a Reuters poll of analysts. The bank, Denmark’s largest, also said it expected a net profit this year of between 18 and 20 billion crowns, down from 20.9 billion in 2017. $1 = 5.9572 Danish crowns Reporting by Teis Jensen
