# Documento Financeiro 81018

February 22, 2018 / 10:31 AM / in 10 minutes Banco do Brasil beats Q4 estimates on lower provisions Reuters Staff 1 Min Read SAO PAULO, Feb 22 (Reuters) - Banco do Brasil beat fourth-quarter profit forecasts as loan loss provisions fell, the state-controlled bank said on Thursday. Recurring net income, which excludes one-time items, jumped 82.5 percent to 3.188 billion reais, topping the 2.804 billion expected by analysts. The Brasilia-based lender’s loan book grew 0.6 percent amid Brazil’s slow economic recovery. (Reporting by Carolina Mandl; editing by Jason Neely)
