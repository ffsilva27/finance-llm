# Documento Financeiro 139899

CAIRO, March 8 (Reuters) - Average yields on Egypt’s six-month and one-year treasury bills fell at an auction on Thursday, data from the central bank showed. The yield on the 182-day bill fell to 17.751 percent from 17.907 percent at the last similar auction, and the yield on the 357-day bill fell to 16.587 percent from 16.894 percent.
