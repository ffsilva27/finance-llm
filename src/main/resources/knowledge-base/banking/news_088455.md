# Documento Financeiro 88455

MADRID (Reuters) - Spain’s Economy Minister Luis de Guindos said he had a clear change of being named the European Central Bank’s vice president after officially announcing his candidature earlier on Wednesday. De Guindos also said he expected Spain to grow more than a growth forecast by the European Union of 2.6 percent in 2018 and reiterated his expectation that economic output would expanded by around 3 percent this year.
