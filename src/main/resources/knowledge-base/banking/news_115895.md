# Documento Financeiro 115895

BEIJING, Feb 9 (Reuters) - Chinese commercial banks’ non-performing loan (NPL) ratio steadied at 1.74 percent at the end of December, unchanged from the end of the third quarter, the country’s banking regulator said on Friday. Non-performing loans total hit 1.71 trillion yuan at end of 2017, while core tier 1 capital adequacy ratio was 10.75 pct, said the regulator at a press conference held in Beijing. (Reporting by Ma Rong in Beijing and Engen Tham in Shanghai;)
