# Documento Financeiro 110162

Feb 14 (Reuters) - Romanian lender Banca Transilvania * Says will start mandatory public tender offer for shares of minority stakeholders of Moldova’s Victoriabank. * Says tender applies to remaining 8.3 million shares, or 33.2 percent of total ordinary nominative shares with voting rights. * Says offered price is 78.21 MDL per share and the mandatory public tender offer is valid for 70 days, during Feb. 14 and April 24. * Banca Transilvania acquired initial indirect stake of 39 percent last year in Victoriabank, the third largest bank in the Republic of Moldova. (Reporting by Luiza Ilie)
