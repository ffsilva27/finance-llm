# Documento Financeiro 115442

MOSCOW, Feb 15 (Reuters) - Russia’s top search site Yandex and Sberbank may complete a deal to set up a joint venture on the basis of Yandex’s e-commerce platform in March, the Interfax news agency Quote: d the state bank’s chief executive as saying on Thursday. The partnership was unveiled in August last year. In December, the companies entered into a binding agreement and said the transaction would likely close in the first half of 2018.
