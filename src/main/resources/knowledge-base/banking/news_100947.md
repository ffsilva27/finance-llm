# Documento Financeiro 100947

Stephen Cutler, a longtime top lawyer and senior executive at JPMorgan Chase & Co., is leaving the bank to join Simpson Thacher & Bartlett LLP as a partner in New York later this year, the law firm is expected to announce Wednesday. Mr. Cutler has spent 11 years with JPMorgan, the majority of those as general counsel. While the bank’s top lawyer, he helped broker a $13 billion settlement related to soured mortgage securities and settled a civil case over a roughly $6 billion trading loss known as the “London whale.” ...
