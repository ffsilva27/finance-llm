# Documento Financeiro 164992

BEIJING—Chinese authorities are cutting the amount of cash banks are required to set aside to cover bad loans, a move that would boost lenders’ bottom line but potentially increase financial risk. With the sector struggling to boost profits, the country’s banking regulator will lower the provision coverage ratio for commercial banks—a requirement to safeguard their ability to weather losses—to a range of 120%-150%, from the current minimum of 150% of their bad loans, according to people familiar with the situation. ...
