# Documento Financeiro 104517

JAKARTA, Feb 6 (Reuters) - Indonesian retail sales grew 0.7 percent in December from a year earlier, a central bank survey showed on Tuesday, down from November’s figure. Growth was held back by a contraction in sales of household utensils, information and communication equipment, motor vehicles spare parts and accessories, it said. November retail sales grew at 2.5 percent year-on-year, supported by food, beverages and automotive fuels. The survey of 700 retailers in 10 major cities projected that January retail sales would grow 1.4 percent from a year earlier. Reporting by Nilufar Rizki; Editing by Nick Macfie
