# Documento Financeiro 134497

HENDERSON, Ky., March 6, 2018 /PRNewswire/ -- Matt Hunsaker has been promoted from Finance Officer, VP to Finance Officer, First VP. He began his career at Field & Main in 2010 and specializes in financial reporting, modeling, planning, efficiency, and balance sheet management. Hunsaker graduated with a Bachelor's in Science from the University of Evansville in 2009 and got his Master of Business Administration from the University of Southern Indiana in 2014. Hunsaker resides in Henderson with his wife, Nicole, and their 2 children, Lex & Quinn. Debbie Murphy has been promoted to Operations & Wire Transfer Officer, Assistant Vice President. Murphy is an Accredited ACH Professional and a certified Electronic Banking Officer. She started working at Field & Main 38 years ago when it was Ohio Valley National Bank. She currently supervises the Deposit Operations department. Murphy resides in Henderson with her husband, Gary. They have two children and two grandchildren. Field & Main Bank ® is a Kentucky-chartered community bank dedicated to serving Kentucky and Indiana with strong leadership, convenient banking facilities and modern craft banking ® . It was formed through the merger of Ohio Valley Financial Group and BankTrust Financial. Learn more at www.fieldandmain.com . Member FDIC.
