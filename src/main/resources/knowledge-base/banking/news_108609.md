# Documento Financeiro 108609

February 1, 2018 / 8:35 AM / Updated 7 minutes ago Banco Bradesco posts Q4 recurring net income of 4.862 bln reais Reuters Staff 1 Min Read SAO PAULO, Feb 1 (Reuters) - Brazil’s Banco Bradesco reported on Thursday a profit in line with estimates although loan-loss provision expenses came in higher than consensus estimates. Recurring net income, which excludes one-off items, totaled 4.862 billion reais as fee revenue increased and expenses decreased. (Reporting by Carolina Mandl; editing by Jason Neely)
