# Documento Financeiro 146075

March 19, 2018 / 7:29 AM / in an hour First Abu Dhabi Bank denies it manipulated Qatari riyal Reuters Staff 1 Min Read ABU DHABI, March 19 (Reuters) - First Abu Dhabi Bank , the United Arab Emirates’ biggest lender, on Monday denied that it had tried to manipulate the Qatari riyal. “FAB conducts its business in accordance with the highest professional standards and complies with the laws and regulations of the jurisdictions in which it operates. FAB categorically refutes these rumours,” it said in a statement. Letters seen by Reuters showed a law firm working for the central bank of Qatar asked U.S. regulators to investigate FAB’s U.S. subsidiary, accusing it of “bogus” foreign exchange deals designed to harm Qatar’s economy as part of a boycott of Doha by other Gulf states. (Reporting by Stanley Carvalho; Editing by Andrew Torchia)
