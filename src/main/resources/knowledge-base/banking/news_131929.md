# Documento Financeiro 131929

MILWAUKEE, March 7, 2018 /PRNewswire-USNewswire/ -- BloodCenter of Wisconsin, part of Versiti, Inc., has announced a series of quarterly continuing education webinars called BloodFLOW – F urther L earning O n the W eb. Webinar topics include: transfusion medicine, transplantation, hematology and genetics. The first webinar in the series – NGS technology for HLA typing – will take place on Wednesday, April 11, 2018 – 1:00p.m. CST. To register, visit BloodFlow webinars. Jennifer Schiller, Ph.D., D (ABHI) will discuss Next Generation Sequencing (NGS) technology and how it may improve donor selection for hematopoietic cell transplant recipients. Her discussion will lead into the analysis of NGS and how it can assist with virtual cross matching.
