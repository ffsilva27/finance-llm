# Documento Financeiro 89085

Mexican affiliates of M&G USA Corp on Monday charged that Mexico’s export bank is trying to improperly circumvent an agreement staying legal actions in order to investigate the bankrupt plastic maker’s books. M&G Holding and M&G Polimeros in court papers said a “standstill” agreement they have with Banco Nacional de Comercio Exterior falls under Mexican jurisdiction, which should bar a Rule 2004 examination. The bank, better known as Bancomext, wants to investigate matters affecting the finances of the bankrupt U.S. parent company of the two affiliates.
