# Documento Financeiro 68280

Broadcom Ltd. secured as much as $100 billion of debt financing for its hostile bid for Qualcomm Inc. and enlisted two more big private-equity firms, strengthening the chip giant’s hand in the takeover battle. A dozen banks—including Bank of America Corp., Citigroup Inc., Deutsche Bank AG, JPMorgan Chase & Co. and Morgan Stanley—have agreed to provide up to $100 billion of committed credit facilities, including a $5 billion revolver and bridge financing, Broadcom said Monday. ...
