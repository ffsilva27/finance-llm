# Documento Financeiro 125707

NEW YORK, Feb. 27, 2018 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Primewaterview Holdings Limited, a Lagos based vertically integrated holding company to Primewaterview Limited and PMWV Management Services in Nigeria, is pleased to announce a newly reconstituted Board of Directors and the implementation of a leadership succession plan. Effective immediately, PrimewaterView Holdings has appointed five new, highly qualified and experienced independent directors: Hon. Thelma Chineze Osammor, founder and managing general partner of Osammor, Otiono & Co; Mr. Adewale Jones, Managing Partner, Alban Consulting Limited Mr. Eric Babatunde Balogun, Principal consultant at Breft Investment limited Dr. Henrietta Ngozi Onwuegbuzie, Senior Lecturer at Lagos Business School, Pan-Atlantic University Harold Nzekwe, Managing Director at Primewaterview Hodings Limited Tiny Diswai, Chairman of the Board of PrimewaterView Holdings Limited, stated, “We are very honoured to have these highy competent and proficient colleagues to come on Board to take Primewaterview to greater heights. The Board is highly balanced with competencies from business, legal, human resources, corporate finance, corporate governance and the academia. I look forward to working with and interacting with these highly distinguished leaders in their respective fields and I am certain that the cross pollination of ideas and skills is just what the company needs to develop and guide its strategy going forward as well as provide the necessary oversight and guidance to management while unlocking the company’s rea value and realising improved financial performance for the shareholder. We look forward to building the company and its employees and its image as one of the prominent players in the Nigerian economy and the real estate sector specifically. On behalf of the Board and management I would like to pay homage and gratitude to the past board having taken Primewaterview this far and we are delighted and honoured to receive the baton from them and take this journey further..” About Primewaterview Holdings Limited PrimewaterView Holdings is a vertically integrated holding Nigerian company that has a very diverse portfolio of investments in Real Estate, Infrastructure, Healthcare, Mining, Oil & Gas, Retail, Banking, Insurance, Cement, Aviation and Transportation. The group has been in business since founded by HRH Prince Adetunji Ogunwusi in 2005 and it continues to grow inorganically. The group was acquired by Milost Global Inc in partnership with Isilo Capital Partners (Pty) Ltd. For more information info@primewaterhl.com Source:Primewaterview Limited
