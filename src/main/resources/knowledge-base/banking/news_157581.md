# Documento Financeiro 157581

Financier Lynn Tilton on Sunday placed the Zohar investment funds she created under chapter 11 bankruptcy protection, a maneuver designed to quell lawsuits over her collection of distressed companies and realize their full value. Structured as collateralized loan obligations, the three Zohar funds were created by Ms. Tilton to finance her private-equity empire but are now locked in several lawsuits with her over loans made to troubled companies in her portfolio. ...
