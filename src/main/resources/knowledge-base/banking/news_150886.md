# Documento Financeiro 150886

MOSCOW, March 1 (Reuters) - Russian state banks Sberbank and VTB could be hurt if they do not transfer defence sector loans to Promsvyazbank because these loans are toxic, the RIA news agency Quote: d Finance Minister Anton Siluanov as saying. In mid February, Siluanov said that Promsvyazbank would receive up to 1 trillion roubles ($17.6 billion) in defence sector’s loans to be transferred mainly from Sberbank and VTB. (Reporting by Polina Devitt; editing by Jason Neely)
