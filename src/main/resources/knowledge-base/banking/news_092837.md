# Documento Financeiro 92837

WASHINGTON, Feb 15 (Reuters) - The U.S. government announced criminal charges against U.S. Bancorp Thursday, alleging the bank willfully failed to have a sufficient anti-money laundering program. The bank agreed to pay over $500 million in forfeiture and fines to several authorities, including the Treasury Department and Federal Reserve. (Reporting by Pete Schroeder Editing by Chizu Nomiyama)
