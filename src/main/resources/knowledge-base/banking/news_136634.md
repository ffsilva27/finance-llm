# Documento Financeiro 136634

Bankrupt printing company Cenveo Inc has taken the gloves off in its fight with noteholder Brigade Capital Management. Cenveo accused Brigade in court papers on Tuesday of launching a smear campaign rooted in a bitter restructuring talks after the investment firm asked the U.S. Bankruptcy Court to order an independent examiner to probe the company’s affairs.
