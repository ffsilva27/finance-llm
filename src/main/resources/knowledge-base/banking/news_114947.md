# Documento Financeiro 114947

JOHANNESBURG, Feb 6 (Reuters) - Stephen Koseff, the entrepreneur who co-founded Anglo-South African investment bank and asset manager Investec, will step down as chief executive in October, the company said. Koseff has been at helm of the company for four decades. He co-founded the company with Bernard Kantor, who is also stepping down as managing director.
