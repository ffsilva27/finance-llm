# Documento Financeiro 125423

Mattel Inc. posted a steep sales decline during the key holiday quarter hurt by weak demand for several of its preschool brands and the bankruptcy of Toys “R” Us Inc. The maker of Barbie and Hot Wheels said sales fell 12% during the fourth quarter, and posted another big loss during the period, adding to the urgency for Chief Executive Margo Georgiadis to enact a turnaround. The toy maker has now posted four straight years of falling sales, with annual revenue down nearly $1.6 billion during that time. ...
