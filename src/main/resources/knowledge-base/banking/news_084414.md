# Documento Financeiro 84414

February 26, 2018 / 6:28 AM / in 3 hours Dutch bank NIBC to seek Amsterdam IPO - company Reuters Staff 1 Min Read AMSTERDAM, Feb 26 (Reuters) - NIBC, a Hague-based Dutch bank, will seek an initial public offering of shares in Amsterdam, the company said on Monday. Its owner, U.S. private equity firm JC Flowers, will seek to sell a 35 percent stake in the flotation “in the coming weeks”, NIBC said in a statement.
