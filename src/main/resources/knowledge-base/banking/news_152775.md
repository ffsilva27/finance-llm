# Documento Financeiro 152775

U.K. lender Barclays PLC has agreed to pay $2 billion in civil penalties to resolve claims of fraud in the sale of residential-mortgage-backed securities in the run-up to the financial crisis, the Justice Department said on Thursday. The department said the settlement will dismiss a civil action filed in December 2016, which alleged Barclays caused billions of dollars in losses to investors in deals involving over $31 billion of securitized mortgage loans between 2005 and 2007. ...
