# Documento Financeiro 109889

Leading sales executive moves into key sales management role PALM HARBOR, Fla.--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- Nationwide Title Clearing (NTC), the leading post-closing services provider for the nation’s largest financial institutions, investors and servicers, announced today that Debbie Lastoria has been promoted to Vice President of National Sales. Lastoria previously served the company as Vice President of Business Development. “Debbie is a natural fit for this expanded role and will now be responsible for leading the effort to introduce our trademark customer service and support to industry firms in search of the best possible partner,” said John Hillman, NTC’s CEO. “Debbie has been working in this business for over 30 years. She started working in mortgage servicing in 1984 and has the deep industry knowledge required to lead this team. She has been a major driving force in establishing Nationwide Title Clearing’s identity as a top service provider in the mortgage industry.” Lastoria joined NTC more than a decade ago and has been a major contributor to the company’s NTC’s 600% revenue growth since she started. Her strategic and creative thinking has provided solutions outside of NTC’s core product line. In many cases, Lastoria was sought out by clients as the essential solution for developing new services that satisfy specific issues and/or compliance requirements. Some of her most recognized new service achievements are: The Assignment Verification Report, Collateral Storage, Review and Remediation Service, Exception Curative Services, Default Assignment Services and Customized Property Report Solutions. Lastoria has been recognized for her excellence numerous times over the years, including making the Inspiring Women in Business list from Tampa Bay Metro Magazine as well as winning HousingWire’s Women of Influence Award. About NTC Nationwide Title Clearing, Inc. (NTC) was founded in 1991 and has grown into a national leading post-closing services provider for the residential mortgage industry. NTC provides a wide range of services to the nation’s top mortgage lenders, servicers and investors as well as various government entities. NTC’s mission is to deliver Life of Loan services with the highest level of accuracy partnered with the mortgage banking industry to protect homeowners while also preserving the integrity of the nation's land records.
