# Documento Financeiro 85067

MOSCOW, Feb 13 (Reuters) - Russia’s top lender Sberbank has received an offer for its Turkish unit Denizbank, Sberbank’s head German Gref told reporters on Tuesday. He also said that the bank had accumulated 40 billion roubles ($693 million) in net profit from foreign subsidiaries, while the bank had no plans to expand oversees. $1 = 57.7499 roubles Reporting by Tatiana Voronova; writing by Vladimir Soldatkin
