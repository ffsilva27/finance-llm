# Documento Financeiro 111012

February 16, 2018 / 7:32 PM / Updated 2 hours ago REFILE-Calif. man pleads guilty to identity fraud in Mueller probe Reuters Staff 1 Min Read (Fixes typo in headline) WASHINGTON, Feb 16 (Reuters) - A California man has pleaded guilty to identity fraud as part of U.S. special counsel Robert Mueller’s probe into alleged Russian meddling in the 2016 U.S. election, according to court documents released on Friday. Richard Pinedo pleaded guilty to knowingly buying and selling bank account numbers to circumvent online payment security systems between 2014 and 2017, according to a Feb. 12 court filing. The announcement came after the United States separately indicted a number of Russians as part of Mueller’s probe.
