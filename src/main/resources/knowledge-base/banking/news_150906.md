# Documento Financeiro 150906

SAO PAULO, March 26 (Reuters) - Brazilian state-controlled oil company Petróleo Brasileiro SA said on Monday it has hired a 2 billion real ($606 million) credit line from state lender Banco do Brasil SA expiring in Feb. 2023. In a securities filing, Petrobras, as the company is known, said the newly-contracted credit line will cost 0.40 percent a year. $1 = 3.30 reais Reporting by Bruno Federowski
