# Documento Financeiro 157953

Congratulations to Barclays PLC, which was vindicated Thursday for its December 2016 decision to fight the Obama Administration’s attempt to stage one last bank robbery. The British bank agreed to pay $2 billion to settle the Justice Department charges without admitting guilt. The $2 billion is the amount Barclays had previously told Justice was the most it would pay, and it is much less than the $5 billion Justice demanded. It is also much less than the $7 billion that Deutsche Bank and $5 billion that Credit Suisse paid...
