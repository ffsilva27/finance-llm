# Documento Financeiro 149927

NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- Lehman Brothers Holdings Inc. (“LBHI”), as Plan Administrator, announced today in a court filing the percentage recovery that will be distributed on April 5, 2018 to holders of allowed claims against LBHI and its various affiliated Debtors (collectively, “Lehman”). Lehman’s aggregate fifteenth distribution to unsecured creditors pursuant to its confirmed chapter 11 plan will total approximately $2.6 billion. This distribution includes (1) $2.3 billion of payments on account of third-party claims, which includes non-controlled affiliate claims, and (2) $0.4 billion of payments among the Lehman Debtors and their controlled affiliates (see Exhibit B to the court filing, Docket # 57808, for further detail). Cumulatively through the fifteenth distribution, Lehman’s total distributions to unsecured creditors will amount to approximately $124.6 billion including (1) $92.2 billion of payments on account of third-party claims, which includes non-controlled affiliate claims, and (2) $32.4 billion of payments among the Lehman Debtors and their controlled affiliates. In accordance with the chapter 11 plan, which was confirmed on December 6, 2011, and subject to available funds, the Lehman Debtors’ sixteenth distribution to creditors is anticipated to be made within 5 business days of September 30, 2018. The chapter 11 plan, related disclosure statement and other filings, including the filing referred to above, can be found at www.lehman-docket.com in the “Key Documents” section. Questions relating to the distribution can be directed to the Debtors’ claims agent, Epiq Systems, Inc., at 1-866-879-0688 (U.S.) and 1-503-597-7691 (Non-U.S.).
