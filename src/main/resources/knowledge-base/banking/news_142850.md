Deutsche Bank prices DWS listing at €32.50 a share Offering price for partial float of fund management business is toward lower end of range By Jenny Strasburg March 23, 2018 Updated: 1:01 p.m. GMT Deutsche Bank said on Thursday it expects to receive about €1.4bn ($1.7bn) from selling a minority stake in its asset-management business, DWS Group, after pricing shares toward the lower end of a range announced earlier this month. The lender placed 44.5 million shares at €32.50 a share, valuing the business at €6.5bn. It had announced an offering range of €30 to €36 a share on March 11.
