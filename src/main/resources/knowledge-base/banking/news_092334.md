# Documento Financeiro 92334

MILAN, Feb 8 (Reuters) - Italy’s biggest bank UniCredit said on Thursday its turnaround plan was making progress after it posted a 2017 net profit of 3.7 billion euros ($4.5 billion) net of extraordinary items, from an 11.8 billion euro loss the previous year. After one-offs including asset disposals, net profit was 5.5 billion euros, just ahead of a 5.2 billion euro analyst consensus provided by the company. The bank said its fully-loaded core capital ratio stood at 13.02 percent on a pro-forma basis taking account of the introduction of the IFRS9 accounting rule and of a large bad loan disposal carried out by the bank. It said it estimated the IFRS9 impact would be negative for 78 basis points on its fully-loaded core capital ratio before taxes. $1 = 0.8146 euros Reporting by Valentina Za, editing by Stephen Jewkes
