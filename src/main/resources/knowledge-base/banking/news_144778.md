March 26, 2018 / 8:25 AM / Updated 11 minutes ago China names Guo Shuqing central bank Communist Party chief, vice governor - PBOC publication Reuters Staff 1 Min Read BEIJING (Reuters) - Guo Shuqing, head of China’s new regulator for the banking and insurance sectors, has been appointed the Communist Party chief and deputy governor of the People’s Bank of China (PBOC), Financial News, a PBOC publication, said on Monday.
