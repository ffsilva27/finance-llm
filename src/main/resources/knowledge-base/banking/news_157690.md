# Documento Financeiro 157690

March 29 (Reuters) - Bank Polska Kasa Opieki SA (Bank Pekao SA): * Poland’s Bank Pekao says it plans to pay out a dividend of 7.9 zloty ($2.31) per share or 99.3 percent of its 2017 unconsolidated net profit * The total amount of the dividend will be 2.074 billion zloty * The planned dividend record date will be July 6, while the actual pay out will be conducted on July 20 * A year earlier dividend amounted to 8.68 zloty per share.
