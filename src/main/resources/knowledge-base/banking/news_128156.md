# Documento Financeiro 128156

BUENOS AIRES, Feb 8 (Reuters) - Argentina’s Grupo Financiero Galicia SA posted a net profit of 2.676 bln pesos ($135.6 million) in the fourth quarter of 2017, up more than 50 percent from 1.772 billion pesos in the same period a year earlier. Grupo Financiero Galicia, which controls Argentina’s largest domestic bank by loans and deposits Banco Galicia, posted full-year 2017 profit of 8.329 billion pesos, up from 6.018 billion pesos a year earlier. $1 = 19.74 pesos Reporting by Walter Bianchi and Caroline Stauffer
