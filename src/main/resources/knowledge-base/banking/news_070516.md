# Documento Financeiro 70516

(Reuters) - Indian shares ended lower on Monday, with sentiment hurt as state-run lenders extended losses over worries about a $1.77 billion fraud disclosed by Punjab National Bank last week that has a bearing on some other banks. The benchmark BSE Sensex closed down 0.69 percent at 33,774.66. The broader NSE Nifty ended 0.71 percent lower at 10,378.40. Punjab National Bank fell for the fourth straight day and closed 8.3 percent lower. Reporting by Tanvi Mehta in Bengaluru; Editing by Sunil Nair Our Standards: The Thomson Reuters Trust Principles.
