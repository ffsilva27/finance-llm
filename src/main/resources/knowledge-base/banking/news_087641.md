# Documento Financeiro 87641

DALLAS, Feb. 1, 2018 /PRNewswire/ -- The business and entertainment law firm Shackelford, Bowen, McKinley & Norton, LLP, is proud to announce the promotion of Dallas attorney Michelle Snedden to equity partner and Dallas attorney Stephen Shellenberger and Nashville attorney Lauren Kilgore to non-equity partner. "This firm is built upon on a broad foundation, which is evident by the diverse practices of these three attorneys," said founding partner John Shackelford. "Their contributions have been key to this firm's continued success, and we are pleased to recognize their work through these promotions." To learn more about Shackelford, Bowen, McKinley & Norton's lawyers, visit: http://shackelfordlaw.net/attorney-index/ . Ms. Snedden represents real estate owners and affordable housing developers in transactional matters involving low-income housing tax credits, historic tax credits, HOME Funds, tax-exempt bonds, and private and governmental financing sources. With extensive knowledge of HUD programs, she regularly represents FHA-approved lenders. Mr. Shellenberger's practice is focused on business and commercial litigation, as well as family law, wills, trusts and probate matters, personal injury and consumer protection matters. He previously worked with the U.S. Drug Enforcement Administration and assisted the U.S. Attorney's Office for the Eastern District of Texas with the successful prosecution of drug and money laundering organizations in North and East Texas. Ms. Kilgore is a highly respected Nashville-based litigator and entertainment attorney who represents a broad and diverse clientele of individuals and companies in commercial litigation. She also represents musicians, record labels and songwriters in recording, producing, distribution, publishing and incorporation matters. Shackelford, Bowen, McKinley & Norton, LLP is an aviation, general business and entertainment law firm with attorneys and offices in Dallas, Fort Worth and Austin, Texas, and Nashville, Tennessee. The firm represents aircraft owners, operators and servicers; financial institutions; real estate owners and developers; automobile dealerships; entertainment enterprises including artists, publishers, producers, managers and labels; gas transporters; energy companies; and other businesses in legal matters across the country. Learn more about the firm at http://www.shackelfordlaw.net .
