# Documento Financeiro 150458

A federal judge in Brooklyn sentenced Martin Shkreli to seven years in prison on Friday, following the former pharmaceutical executive’s conviction last summer on securities-fraud charges. Federal prosecutors had sought a prison term of at least 15 years for Mr. Shkreli, while his lawyers had asked U.S. District Judge Kiyo A. Matsumoto to impose a sentence of 12 to 18 months in prison. Mr. Shkreli will receive credit for the six months he has served in detention since September. ...
