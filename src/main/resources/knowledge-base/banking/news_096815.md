# Documento Financeiro 96815

MOSCOW, Feb 1 (Reuters) - Changes in the controlling shareholders at Russian banks should be subject to checks by the central bank, Russia’s Central Bank First Deputy Governor Sergei Shvetsov said on Thursday. He said the rule also should apply to financial companies. (Reporting by Elena Fabrichnaya,; writing by Maria Tsvetkova, editing by Larry King) Our Standards: The Thomson Reuters Trust Principles.
