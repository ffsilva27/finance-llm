# Documento Financeiro 159964

YEREVAN, March 27 (Reuters) - Armenia’s central bank said on Tuesday it had decided to keep its key refinancing rate unchanged at 6 percent. Armenia’s consumer price index fell 0.5 percent in February in month-on-month terms, but rose by 3.3 percent year-on-year. That compares with a rise in the CPI of 2.7 percent in January in month-on-month terms and of 2.7 percent in year-on-year terms as well. (Reporting by Hasmik Mkrtchyan; writing by Margarita Antidze; editing by Katya Golubkova)
