# Documento Financeiro 154388

March 26 (Reuters) - The value of assets held by China’s banking sector rose 8.0 percent year-on-year to 248.34 trillion yuan ($39.34 trillion) as of end-February, China Banking Regulatory Commission said on Monday. Liabilities rose 7.7 percent from a year earlier to 228.65 trillion yuan, it said on its website. $1 = 6.3120 Chinese yuan renminbi Reporting by Hong Kong newsroom Editing by Shri Navaratnam
