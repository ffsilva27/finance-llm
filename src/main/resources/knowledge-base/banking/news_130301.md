# Documento Financeiro 130301

PORT LOUIS, Feb 26 (Reuters) - An unidentified investor is preparing to buy a stake in Mauritius’ MauBank, which was formed by the merger of two banks bailed out by the government, its CEO said on Monday. “It is up to the shareholders to agree on the amount and what stakes they are ready to sell,” Shridar Nagarajan, CEO of MauBank told Reuters.
