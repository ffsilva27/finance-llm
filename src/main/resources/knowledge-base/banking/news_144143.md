# Documento Financeiro 144143

March 5 (Reuters) - Standard Chartered appointed Freddy Boom as head of industrials, services and transportation, international corporates for its North America unit. Boom, based in New York, will advise industrials, services and transportation clients. He most recently served as managing director for technology, media and telecom, corporate banking at JPMorgan Chase. (Reporting by Arunima Banerjee in Bengaluru)
