# Documento Financeiro 120358

KUWAIT, Feb 21 (Reuters) - National Bank of Kuwait and Kuwait Finance House on Wednesday signed a 249.2 million dinars ($831.3 million) syndicated loan facility for Turkey’s business group Limak for the building of a new terminal at Kuwait airport. Limak Construction and its partner Kharafi National are building the new terminal at the Kuwait International Airport at a cost of $4.34 billion, which will triple the capacity of the airport to 25 million passengers a year. $1 = 0.2998 Kuwaiti dinars Reporting by Ahmed Hagagy, Writing by Saeed Azhar; Editing by Tom Arnold
