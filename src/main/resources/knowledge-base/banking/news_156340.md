# Documento Financeiro 156340

Real estate giant Brookfield Asset Management Inc. agreed to buy a 25% stake in the European private-debt investor LCM Partners Ltd. and its loan-servicing arm. LCM, which is based in London, manages and services about €25 billion ($31 billion) in assets ranging from credit-card debt and car loans to mortgages. Brookfield oversees $285 billion in assets from headquarters in New York and Toronto, making it one of the world’s largest investors in real estate, infrastructure and private equity. ...
