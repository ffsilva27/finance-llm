# Documento Financeiro 76275

LOS ANGELES, Feb. 13, 2018 /PRNewswire/ -- As the entertainment industry undergoes continued scrutiny, the need increases substantially for savvy attorneys who know how to proactively care for clients, says Zev Raben , who today joined Ramo Law PC from Lavely & Singer P.C. "The entertainment world needs trusted advisors in this time of heightened focus," says Raben, who brings a sharp eye for drafting language to protect clients from productions, distributions and deals that could potentially go sideways. "My experience litigating entertainment disputes provides me unique insight when drafting agreements to avoid future disputes." " Elsa Ramo has built a tremendous organization," said Raben. The firm provides comprehensive legal services to producers, financiers, and owners and creators of film, television and digital content. Just last year, Ramo Law represented several indie films featured at the Toronto Film Festival and 15 films at Sundance, and represents clients in three Oscar-nominated films, including "Call Me By Your Name," "The Disaster Artist" and "Loving Vincent." Television and film credits include the upcoming Mel Gibson thriller "Dragged Across Concrete" and the Netflix docuseries, "Chef's Table." "Zev's litigation experience is an invaluable, complementary addition to our team and will prove to be a tremendous resource to our clients," said Ramo. Raben, a Toronto native who obtained his Bachelor of Arts from McGill University and his J.D. from Benjamin N. Cardozo School of Law, lives in Los Angeles with his wife and their two children. He is licensed to practice in California and New York. Ramo Law PC provides comprehensive legal services to entertainment industry clients, representing financiers, producers, and production entities in film, television and digital content. The firm provides experienced legal services to optimize its clients' financial, legal and business positions in the financing, production and exploitation of their content.
