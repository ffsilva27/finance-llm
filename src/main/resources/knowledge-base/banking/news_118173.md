# Documento Financeiro 118173

February 1, 2018 / 9:41 AM / in 42 minutes Russia's Alfa Bank not considering purchase of Vozrozhdenie Bank - co-owner Reuters Staff 1 Min Read MOSCOW, Feb 1 (Reuters) - Russia’s Alfa Bank is not considering the purchase of Vozrozhdenie Bank, Alfa Bank co-owner Mikhail Fridman said on Thursday. He added that he may consider buying Eurobonds planned by the finance ministry to repatriate some of the funds back home from abroad if conditions are attractive.
