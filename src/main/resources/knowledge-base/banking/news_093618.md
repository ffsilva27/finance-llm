February 4, 2018 / 12:38 PM / in 2 hours Israel legalizes West Bank outpost after settler killed Reuters Staff 2 Min Read JERUSALEM (Reuters) - Israel granted legal status on Sunday to a previously unauthorized outpost in the occupied West Bank in response to a Palestinian shooting attack last month that killed one of its residents. Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu said the decision to retroactively authorize the 50-family outpost of Havat Gilad was meant to “allow the continuation of normal life there”. Most countries deem Israeli settlements in the West Bank, land that Israel captured in a 1967 war and which Palestinians want for a future state, to be illegal. Israel disputes this. The decision was approved unanimously by Israel’s cabinet.
