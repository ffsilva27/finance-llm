# Documento Financeiro 122776

February 2nd, 2018 South America A federal court on Thursday ordered the arrest of the former chief executive officer of Bank of New York Mellon Corp’s unit in Brazil, a source with knowledge of the matter said. The court in Brasilia issued the order to detain José Carlos Xavier de Oliveira, said the source, who spoke on condition of anonymity. The court declined to comment. All proceedings are under seal. BNY Mellon declined to comment on the arrest order. The full original article can be found at reuters.com
