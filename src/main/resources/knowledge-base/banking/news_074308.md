# Documento Financeiro 74308

OTTAWA—Canadian manufacturing sales slid in December after reaching a record level in the previous month, falling short of expectations as petroleum and coal product sales fell. Factory sales declined 0.3% in December from the previous month to a seasonally adjusted 55.52 billion Canadian dollars ($44.39 billion), Statistics Canada said Friday. The expectation among traders was for an increase in manufacturing sales of 0.3%, according to economists from Royal Bank of Canada. ...
