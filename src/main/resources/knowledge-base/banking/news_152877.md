# Documento Financeiro 152877

JAKARTA (Reuters) - Perry Warjiyo, who has been nominated to become Bank Indonesia (BI) governor, told a parliamentary hearing on Wednesday that the central bank can support both economic growth and financial stability at the same time. Warjiyo, currently a deputy BI governor, also said macroprudential rules need to be relaxed to spur lending growth, and that he wants banks to buy more corporate bonds. He did not elaborate. He said Southeast Asia’s largest economy will likely grow 5.2-5.3 percent in 2018, fractionally above last year’s pace. Warjiyo is the sole nominee proposed for central bank governor by President Joko Widodo. If Warjiyo’s nomination is approved by parliament, he will succeed Agus Martowardojo, whose five-year term as governor ends in May.
