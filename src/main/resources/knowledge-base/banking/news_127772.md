# Documento Financeiro 127772

Credit-rating agency Standard & Poor's on Wednesday cut Wells Fargo 's credit ratings, citing further regulatory and governance risks. The agency downgraded Wells Fargo's long-term issuer credit rating to "A-" from "A" and short term issuer credit rating to "A-2" from "A-1". Regulatory risk for Wells is more severe than previously expected and the process for improving its governance and operational risk policies may take longer than previously expected, S&P said in a statement.
