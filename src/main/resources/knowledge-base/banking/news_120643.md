# Documento Financeiro 120643

TUNIS, Feb 15 (Reuters) - Tunisia’s parliament approved the appointment of World Bank official Marouane el Abassi as the new central bank governor on Thursday. The appointment came a day after former central bank governor Chedli Ayari resigned. (Reporting By Tarek Amara; Editing by Andrew Heavens)
