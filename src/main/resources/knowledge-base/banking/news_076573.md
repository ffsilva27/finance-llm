# Documento Financeiro 76573

Feb 26 (Reuters) - * India’s cenbank on exchange traded FX derivatives: allows foreign investors to take positions without underlying exposure of up to $100 million across all currency pairs involving rupee * India cenbank: currently foreign investors can take short or long positions up to $15 mln in USD/INR pair per exchange without underlying exposure * India cenbank: foreign investors can also take short or long positions in EUR/INR, GBP/INR and JPY/INR pairs for total of up to $5 mln per exchange without underlying exposure
