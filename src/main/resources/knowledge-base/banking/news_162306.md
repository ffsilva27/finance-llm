# Documento Financeiro 162306

TOKYO (Reuters) - Bank of Japan Executive Director Masayoshi Amamiya said on Monday the benefits of the central bank’s massive monetary stimulus were outweighing the side effects. Masayoshi Amamiya, a nominee for Bank of Japan deputy governor, attends a confirmation hearing in the lower house of parliament in Tokyo Japan March 5, 2018. REUTERS/Toru Hanai Amamiya, a nominee for one of the two BOJ deputy governor posts, told a confirmation hearing in parliament’s lower house that the bank would scrutinize the costs and benefits in guiding monetary policy.
