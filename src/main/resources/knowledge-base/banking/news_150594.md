# Documento Financeiro 150594

BRUSSELS (Reuters) - The euro zone’s bailout fund approved on Tuesday the disbursement of 6.7 billion euros ($8.3 billion) in new loans to Greece as part of its current bailout program, it said in a statement. Euro coins are seen in front of a displayed Greece flag in this picture illustration, June 29, 2015. REUTERS/Dado Ruvic/File Photo The decision confirms a political deal reached by the euro zone’s finance ministers earlier in March. A first tranche of 5.7 billion euros is due to be paid on Wednesday, while the remainder will be disbursed after May 1, the European Stability Mechanism said. ($1 = 0.8070 euros) Reporting Francesco Guarascio, Editing by Gabriela Baczynska
