# Documento Financeiro 86993

February 19, 2018 / 5:17 PM / Updated 8 minutes ago Latvian c.bank chief Rimsevics released from custody Reuters Staff 1 Min Read RIGA, Feb 19 (Reuters) - Latvia’s anti-corruption agency released central bank Governor Ilmars Rimsevics from custody on Monday after accusing him of soliciting a bribe. Video footage from Latvian news portal Delfi showed Rimsevics rejecting the allegations as he was released. “I disagree with it categorically,” Rimsevics told reporters.
