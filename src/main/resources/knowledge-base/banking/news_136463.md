# Documento Financeiro 136463

Feb 28 (Reuters) - The following financial services industry appointments were announced on Wednesday. To inform us of other job changes, email moves@thomsonreuters.com. SABRE INSURANCE GROUP PLC UK-based Sabre appointed Andy Pomfret as senior independent director. CREDIT AGRICOLE Credit Agricole has hired George Kalbin to work on its financial institutions syndicate desk, based in London, according to a source familiar with the matter. CREDIT SUISSE Credit Suisse named Paul Galietto head of global prime services to replace Indrajit Bardhan, who is leaving the bank to pursue outside opportunities, according to a memo obtained by IFR. (Compiled by Uday Sampath in Bengaluru)
