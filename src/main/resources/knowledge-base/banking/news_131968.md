# Documento Financeiro 131968

March 22, 2018 / 5:13 PM / Updated 26 minutes ago MOVES-Moelis names Joel Thompson managing director Reuters Staff 1 Min Read March 22 (Reuters) - Investment bank Moelis & Co said on Thursday it had named Joel Thompson managing director, providing financial and strategic advice to medical technology companies. Thompson joins the firm in May and will be based in New York. Thompson was most recently a managing director at Morgan Stanley, where he led medical technology investment banking coverage. (Reporting by Arunima Banerjee in Bengaluru)
