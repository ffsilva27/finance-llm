# Documento Financeiro 105699

(Recasts to lead with cause of trade surplus) SANTIAGO, Feb 7 (Reuters) - Chile posted a trade surplus of $1.21 billion in January, thanks in part to a sharp rise in copper exports, central bank data showed on Wednesday. Copper exports increased by 7.0 percent over the same month the previous year, boosted by rising prices of the red metal. Copper prices jumped more than 30 percent in 2017, sharply boosting export revenues in the world's top copper producer.
