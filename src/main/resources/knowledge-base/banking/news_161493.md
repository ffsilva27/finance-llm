# Documento Financeiro 161493

MEXICO CITY, March 5 (Reuters) - Mexican construction company ICA said on Monday that a judge had approved its creditors agreement, ending the group’s bankruptcy proceedings. ICA suspended debt payments at the end of 2015 after a crash in the peso made its dollar-denominated debt load unbearable. The company entered into bankruptcy proceedings in September last year. (Reporting by Christine Murray; Editing by Frank Jack Daniel)
