# Documento Financeiro 88821

NEW YORK, Feb 16 (Reuters) - Citigroup Inc raised Chief Executive Officer Michael Corbat’s annual compensation by 48 percent to $23 million for 2017, a year in which the bank made more money from operations but still fell short of earlier targets. Directors considered recent progress toward new targets when setting Corbat’s pay, according to a public filing on Friday. (Reporting by David Henry in New York)
