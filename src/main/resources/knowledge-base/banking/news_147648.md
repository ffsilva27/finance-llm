# Documento Financeiro 147648

BEIJING, March 9 (Reuters) - China’s central bank governor Zhou Xiaochuan said there are liquidity risks in financial holding companies that lack transparency. The People’s Bank of China will strengthen management on internal dealings at financial holding companies, Zhou said, speaking on the sidelines of the annual parliament session. (Reporting by Kevin Yao, Se Young Lee and Yawen Chen;) Our Standards: The Thomson Reuters Trust Principles.
