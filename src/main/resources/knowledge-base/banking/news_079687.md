# Documento Financeiro 79687

KUALA LUMPUR, Feb 6 (Reuters) - Malaysia's gross international reserves rose slightly to $103.7 billion as of Jan. 30, up from $103.0 billion as of Jan. 15, the central bank said on Tuesday. Bank Negara Malaysia (BNM) said the reserves were sufficient to finance 7.2 months of retained imports, and were 1.1 times the short-term external debt.
